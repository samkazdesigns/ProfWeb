P-02 · MEDICAL DEVICES · SEP 2023 — MAY 2024

Earthworm-Inspired Soft Body Robot

Senior capstone: a vein-injectable soft robot concept designed to travel within brain vasculature and perform thrombectomy — reducing required procedural staff by 50% and lowering treatment costs for hospitals.

ROLE

Team Lead

TIMELINE

Sep 2023 — May 2024

TEAM

TOOLS

FIG. 1

01 — OVERVIEW

Overview

The objective was to create a scaled-up earthworm-like soft body robot that could replace the necessity of thrombolysis: a vein-injectable robot navigated to the blood clot location to perform mechanical thrombectomy.

02 — OBJECTIVE & CONSTRAINTS

Objective & Constraints

Although a large-scale prototype was built, the design had to remain viable within the small blood vessels of the brain. Conventional actuation methods such as motors were ruled out — shape-memory alloy (Nitinol) actuation was selected instead, with biocompatibility as a governing requirement.
