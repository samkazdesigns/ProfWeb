Autonomous Navigation Robotic Boat

01 — OVERVIEW

Overview

Created successful iterations of a robotic boat capable of autonomously capturing and retrieving objects within a body of water, using ARUCO marker tracking for object location.

Objective & Constraints

The boat had to stay stable and maneuverable while carrying its capture payload, demanding hull design validated for buoyancy under added load.
